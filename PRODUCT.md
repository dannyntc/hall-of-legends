# Hall of Legends

## Product truth

Hall of Legends is an immersive single-screen experience where legendary characters from different fictional universes share a sacred sanctuary and one piece of wisdom each.

## Audience and scene

Visitors arrive looking for a memorable, calming encounter with characters they admire. The experience should feel like entering a quiet mythic place, then choosing one figure for a personal moment.

## First surface

The first surface is the sanctuary tableau. It must communicate the gathering immediately, keep Naruto Uzumaki, Monkey D. Luffy, Roronoa Zoro, and Bell Cranel visible, and make selection discoverable without introducing card grids or application chrome.

## Constraints

Frontend-only, English, no autoplay audio, keyboard accessible, touch usable, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. The initial release uses generated visual assets and short attributed lines with source links.
